BRANCH: development
===================

OBJECTIVE
---------
Provide a backed-up integration area for new nephron model work before it is
validated and merged into main.

CONTENTS
--------
- Everything currently available on main.
- New solver changes, mesh experiments, and documentation improvements that
  are still being developed or validated.

RULES
-----
- Keep generated meshes and solver outputs out of Git.
- Commit work frequently with descriptive messages and push this branch.
- Run syntax checks and representative small simulations before merging.
- Merge validated work to main; do not force-push either shared branch.
