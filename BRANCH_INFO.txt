BRANCH: main
============

OBJECTIVE
---------
Provide the stable, organized, reviewable version of the nephron model source.

CONTENTS
--------
- Active transport, flow, and coupled Lua models under code/.
- Active and archived mesh-generation tools under mesh_generation/.
- Compact grids and nephron traces needed to reproduce workflows.
- Documentation explaining commands, branches, layout, and design decisions.

RULES
-----
- Keep generated meshes and solver outputs out of Git.
- Make new work on the development branch, validate it, then merge to main.
- Do not rewrite published main history.
- Use the dated archive branch to recover the pre-organization layout.
